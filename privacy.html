Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Who this covers

"Slow Rooms Uploader" (the app) is a private tool run by the owner of the Slow Rooms YouTube channel. Its only user is the channel owner, who signs in with their own Google account to upload and schedule videos on their own channel.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

  • Permission to upload videos and thumbnails and to edit the title, description, tags and publishing schedule of videos on the signed-in channel.
  • Read-only channel and video information (channel name, video status, view, like and comment counts) used to confirm the right channel and to keep a private performance log.

The app does not read, collect or store data about viewers or any other YouTube user.

Storage and sharing

The OAuth refresh token and the statistics above are stored only on the channel owner's own computer, in a file readable only by that account. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or sent anywhere other than Google's APIs.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file also stops the app.
